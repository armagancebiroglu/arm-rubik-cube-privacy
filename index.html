Privacy Policy

Application: ARM Rubik Cube

Developer: Armagan Cebiroglu Apps

Effective date: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

ARM Rubik Cube is a puzzle game designed to provide an enjoyable Rubik's Cube solving experience. We aim to minimize the information processed when you use the application.

2. Player Information

ARM Rubik Cube does not require users to provide their real name, email address, phone number, or create an account using personal credentials.

Players may choose a nickname. Nicknames, game scores, completion times, and leaderboard results are stored using Supabase to provide online ranking features.

Player nicknames and game results may be visible to other users on the leaderboard. Please avoid using personal information in your nickname.

3. Camera Access

Camera access is requested only when the player chooses to scan a physical Rubik's Cube.

Camera images are processed locally on the device to identify cube colors. These images are not uploaded to Supabase or other external servers. The application does not retain camera images.

4. Local Storage

The application may store game progress, preferences, and gameplay settings locally on the user's device.

5. Online Database Services

ARM Rubik Cube uses Supabase to store player nicknames and game results required for online leaderboard functionality.

Supabase may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, and maintain its services.

6. Advertising

ARM Rubik Cube does not currently include third-party advertising services.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the advertising provider and any associated data processing.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell player information. Player nicknames and scores may be displayed publicly through the application's leaderboard. Supabase provides the database infrastructure used for this feature.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect application data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data can be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it. This does not automatically delete online leaderboard records.

Players may request the deletion of their online nickname and associated game records by contacting the developer using the support contact information in the application's Google Play listing.

10. Children's Privacy

ARM Rubik Cube does not require players to submit their real names or contact details. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online leaderboard features.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, please contact the developer using the support contact information provided in the Google Play listing for ARM Rubik Cube.
